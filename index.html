Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions regarding campus registration, participation rules, or downloading your digital pass?

How do I receive my registration pass and ticket?
Upon submitting the event registration form, your digital boarding pass is instantly generated with a unique Booking Reference Code and an SVG QR Code. You can print or save it as a PDF, add it directly to your calendar, or retrieve it anytime under the "My Tickets" section in the navigation bar.
Are students from other universities allowed to participate?
Yes! Flagship events like HackSprint X, Kalliope ’26 Cultural Fest, and the Esports Championship welcome participants from colleges across India. Select "External University" in the registration form and bring your valid college ID card to the venue.
Can I cancel or manage my booked events?
Yes. Simply click on the "My Tickets" button in the navigation header. You can view all your stored registrations in Local Storage, print existing passes, or cancel any registration with a single click to free up seats for other attendees.
Will certificates and merchandise be provided?
All registered participants attending technical conclaves, hackathons, and certified workshops receive official verified certificates along with free swag kits, snacks, and access to networking zones.
