Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my verified event pass?

Upon submitting the registration form, your digital boarding pass is instantly generated with a unique Booking Reference Code and an animated SVG QR Code. You can print it as a clean PDF, add it directly to your calendar, or retrieve it anytime under the "My Passes" button in the top navigation.

Can external students from other universities participate?

Yes! Flagship events like HackSprint X, Kalliope ’26 Cultural Carnival, TEDxKIIT, and the Esports Tournament welcome delegates from colleges across India. Select "Other External University" during registration and carry your valid college ID.

How does the pass cancellation work?

Click on "My Passes" in the top bar to inspect your registered tickets stored in your browser. You can cancel any pass with one click to release seats back into the live attendee pool.
